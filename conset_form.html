Introduction

This is a research study on how people produce and comprehend language. The purpose of this study is to reveal how humans use and understand different languages and develop research and pedagogical linguistic resources for different languages. The name of this research study is "Language and Speech Investigation in Different Languages". I am seeking your participation in this study.

Your participation is completely voluntary, and I will address your questions or concerns at any point before or during the study. You may be eligible to participate in this study if you meet the following criteria:

  • You are over 18 years old; 2
  • You are a native speaker of languages like English, Chinese, Spanish or other local languages varieties.

If you decide to participate in this study, you will be asked to do the following activities:

  • Answer some questions about your language background and/or experience.
  • Language consultation sessions over zoom: you will be asked about some questions on your language.
  • Language production and comprehension experiments: Read/listen to sentences [[in some versions: and look at pictures]] and answer questions about them.

The total duration is expected to be approximately 60-120 minutes per session, conducted daily over a period of 1-2 months. After or before you complete the study, we will meet regularly over zoom or in person for language consultation. During the session, we may also have language production and perception experiments, with each one lasting about around 10-60 minutes. you will receive a digital gift card worth range between approximately $15 or $15 cash per hour; the amount will be computed relative to the duration of the study. For specific language experiments, you will receive payment approximately $2.50-$12.00, the exact amount will be calculated based on the study duration in accordance with Prolific's payment.

The results in my research projects will be published through theses, publications, academic presentations, and proceedings. In addition, de-identified speech recordings and related language data collected in this study will be entered into a research data repository to create speech databases or language tools that may be used by other researchers. Participants will not be identified in the results or databases. We will take reasonable measures to protect the security of all your personal information. All data will be de-identified before any publication, presentation, or inclusion in a data repository. Each participant will be assigned a unique code. A separate, secure file linking the codes to the data repository will be retained, and it will only be accessed if a participant requests to withdraw or have their data deleted.

Please take as much time as you need to read the consent form. You may want to discuss it with your family, friends, or your personal doctor. If you find any of the language difficult to understand, please ask questions. If you decide to participate, you will be asked to sign this form. A copy of the signed form will be provided to you for your records.

Risk and Discomforts

The risks of this study are minimal. Because linguistic data may be shared for research purposes, there is a small risk that someone outside the study could gain access to your identity or personal information.

Some questions or tasks may feel uncomfortable or embarrassing. You may skip any question or stop participating at any time without penalty.

Since data are collected from different language groups, some potential community-level risks include misinterpretation or overgeneralization of findings for language groups, e.g., groups that speak different local varieties of Chinese, Spanish, Yi languages, Maya, Yoruba, etc.

Benefits

There are no direct benefits to you from taking part in this study. However, your participation in this study may help us learn about properties of human language. Publicizing language data can help to develop tools and educational resources for different languages and cultures

Privacy/Confidentiality

All data will be de-identified before any publication, presentation, or inclusion in a data repository. Each participant will be assigned a unique code. A separate, secure file linking the codes to the data repository will be retained, and it will only be accessed if a participant requests to withdraw or have their data deleted.

We will keep your records for this study confidential as far as permitted by law. However, if we are required to do so by law, we will disclose confidential information about you.

Future use of data

This study is collecting data from you. We will make your data available for a dataset. Other research studies that may be done in the future may also use this dataset. The research may be about similar conditions to this study. However, research could also be about unrelated, conditions, or other types of research. These studies may be done by researchers at this institution or other institutions, including commercial entities. Our goal is to make more research possible. We plan to keep your data indefinitely. All data will be transferred securely. If you are not comfortable with this, you should not participate in this study.

Your data and collected as part of this research will not be used or distributed for future research studies without your explicit written permission and the written permission from your community or group.

Alternatives

An alternative would be to not participate in this study.

Payments/Compensation

For the language consultation, you will receive a digital gift card worth range between approximately $15 or $15 cash per hour; the amount will be computed relative to the duration of the study. For specific language experiments, you will receive payment approximately $2.50-$12.00 per hour, the exact amount will be calculated based on the study duration in accordance with Prolific's payment. For online web-based experiments, if a response looks like it was produced by a bot for example, shows no signs of understanding the task, provides nonsense data or if the task is completed in a time span that is impossibly fast for humans - we may not be able to pay the participant for their time. To avoid bots and multiple submissions, an email address can only be used once to enter the raffle. Participation is voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without penalty; compensation/credit will be prorated when applicable.
